Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026.

This policy covers Nudge, including version 2.1. Features described apply when available in your version and enabled.

Who provides Nudge

Nudge is published by Christopher Peluso, an independent U.S.-based app developer. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@nudgestep.com.

Nudge has no user accounts or developer-controlled cloud database. The app does not send your walking history, steps, goals, workouts, or Apple Health records to the developer. Information you choose to email is described below.

See the separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for health-data categories, recipients, requests, and appeals.

Health data on your devices

Core step and workout access and optional additional Health permissions let Nudge read Apple Health steps, walking/running distance, movement and exercise time, walking speed and step length, flights climbed, active energy, workouts and routes, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, and heart-rate variability. It uses these records locally for progress, history, walking estimates, and personalized guidance. iPhone Health access is read-only. Workout place names are resolved on-device; route locations are not sent to a geocoding service. Watch Walk Plan opens Apple’s Workout app to record workouts.

History, goals, settings, and imported Health data are stored on your devices. Paired iPhone and Apple Watch can exchange history, settings, forecasts, and Nudge+ access; widgets use local data. Nudge has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell your health or location data.

Limited external services

These requests go directly to the providers, not through a developer-operated server. They do not send your Apple Health records.

  • Apple weather and location lookup. Walking Forecast sends current or saved forecast coordinates to Apple WeatherKit, including direct Watch requests. iPhone place lookup sends entered places or coordinates to Apple’s geocoding service. Optional background location permission allows travel-related forecast updates. Apple privacy information.
  • Optional NOAA alerts. Additional U.S. alerts are off by default. Enabling them sends the Current or saved Home forecast point, which may be precise, directly to the National Weather Service, including during eligible background updates. NOAA also receives your IP address but no Health data or Nudge account identifier. NOAA privacy notice.
  • Nudge+ subscriptions. Apple handles purchases, payments, refunds, and restoration. Nudge stores verified product, transaction and app-specific account identifiers, purchase dates, and expiry information locally to determine access. App Store privacy information.

Retention and backups

iPhone daily summaries, goals, imported hourly steps, saved forecast locations, and general forecast archives have no fixed expiry. Watch daily and weekly history normally covers the current and three previous calendar weeks. When stored files are accessible, Nudge prunes walking-bout details after 90 days, workout details and associated forecast copies after one year, and sleep and heart aggregates after 13 months.

Nudge marks working Health-derived history and caches for exclusion from backups. A separate recovery record may contain selected settings, plans, eligible goals, and deletion records, but not imported steps, workouts, sleep, or heart readings. Apple Health’s own sync and backups follow your Apple settings.

Your choices and deletion

  • Change or withdraw Health and location permissions in Apple’s settings. Turn off Walking Forecast, background location, or Additional U.S. alerts in Nudge. Disabling NOAA alerts stops future automatic lookups; downloaded alerts may remain until expiry. Withdrawing permission does not erase stored history or Apple Health records.
  • In Settings → History & Privacy, Delete Nudge History removes daily and weekly history and queues deletion for your paired Watch’s next sync. Plans, goals, settings, feedback, forecast archives, and other local stores are separate.
  • Clear Imported Health Context clears imported Health snapshots. With permission, Nudge may import data again; Rebuild All History from Apple Health explicitly rebuilds step history. These controls do not delete original Apple Health records.
  • Exports create local files; you choose whether and with whom to share them. Deletion in Nudge does not remove exported files or shared copies, older backups, or records already held by external providers.

Support and privacy requests

Emailing voluntarily shares your email address, message, and any attachments with Christopher Peluso to respond to your request. Please avoid including Health records, full history exports, or precise locations. Email hello@nudgestep.com for access, correction, deletion, withdrawal, or other privacy requests concerning information you have provided. To appeal a refusal, reply with “Privacy appeal.” The Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy explains the request and appeal process and local-data limits.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests and resolve related issues.
