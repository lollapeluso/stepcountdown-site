Proposed Nudge 2.1 privacy policy

Proposed policy — pending final approval. Not yet effective.

This Nudge 2.1 policy is published on the preview website for review only. It does not replace the policy for the current Nudge 1.3 release. No effective date has been set.

Operator identity, business contact details where required, support-provider and retention practices, and applicable legal requirements still need confirmation. These open items are identified below.

This proposed policy describes how Nudge 2.1 handles information when you use its walking, activity, weather, and subscription features. It is prepared for review ahead of the U.S. launch.

Effective date: To be set after final approval.

Health, activity, and coaching

With your Apple Health permission, Nudge reads step counts, walking and running distance, movement and exercise time, walking speed and step length, flights climbed, active energy, workouts and workout routes, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, and heart-rate variability. Nudge uses these data to show progress and history, estimate walking time, and personalize walking guidance and reminders. Nudge can look up a place name for a workout with a usable route start. This lookup takes place on your iPhone with bundled place data and sends no workout location to a geocoding service. Nudge’s current Watch Walk Plan opens a suggested walk in Apple’s Workout app, which handles workout recording. With your Health permission, Nudge can read workouts recorded there. The iPhone’s Health access is read-only.

Apple Health access is optional. If you limit or withdraw access, the features that use those records may show less history or guidance. You can manage what Nudge can read or write in Apple Health.

Nudge stores activity history, imported health details, goals, and settings on your devices. It can sync step and goal history, settings, forecasts, and Nudge+ access between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch. Widgets use data from Nudge on the device and Apple Health steps where authorized. Nudge schedules its notifications on your devices. You can manage them in Nudge and in device settings. Nudge has no separate Nudge account or developer-operated cloud history service. The services below describe when information can leave your device.

External services and sharing

Feature Information used and why
Walking Forecast Nudge sends current or saved forecast coordinates to Apple WeatherKit to get weather. The Apple Watch can also request a forecast directly from Apple WeatherKit using its location when its independent forecast feature is used. Nudge on iPhone can send an entered place, such as a postal code and country, or coordinates to Apple’s geocoding service to look up a location or its name. With your background location permission, Nudge can respond to significant location changes to update forecasts while you travel. Apple WeatherKit · Apple geocoding
Supplemental U.S. weather alerts If you turn on Additional U.S. alerts, Nudge sends the latitude and longitude of the U.S. forecast point directly to the U.S. National Weather Service (NOAA) to look up alerts. Nudge sends the point as received, which can be precise or approximate depending on its source and your device settings. This can be your Current forecast location or a saved Home location based on your postal code, including when you are away. Eligible automatic updates can run in the background. The service receives your connection’s IP address and request information, including a header identifying Nudge. The request contains no Apple Health data or Nudge account identifier. NOAA’s published website notice describes logging IP addresses and requested resources, possible extended retention for security, and sharing with other U.S. agencies in some circumstances. The notice leaves the logging and retention of coordinates for this endpoint unspecified. National Weather Service API · National Weather Service privacy notice
Nudge+ access Apple handles payments, code redemption, refunds, and restoration through the App Store. Nudge keeps verified information such as the product, purchase and expiry dates, and transaction and app-specific account identifiers on the device to determine feature access, including offline. App Store and privacy
Exports and reviews Preparing an export creates a temporary file on your device with daily summaries and your walking plan. Monthly reviews can be exported as images. When you share a file, you choose its recipient or storage service.
Support and privacy requests If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any device details or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, troubleshoot, and handle your request. Please remove health details and precise locations from screenshots and avoid sending full history exports.

How long Nudge keeps data

The iPhone keeps daily summaries, historical goals, and imported hourly step history for long-term trends without a fixed expiry. The Watch normally keeps daily and weekly history for the current and three previous calendar weeks. Nudge prunes walking-bout details older than 90 days, workout details older than one year, and sleep and heart aggregates older than 13 months when it can access the stored files. The one-year limit also applies to forecast copies saved for specific workouts. Saved forecast locations and the general forecast archive have no fixed expiry. Support retention remains an owner review item: the existing draft proposes keeping support messages as needed to handle the request, record its resolution, or meet legal obligations. The actual retention practice and email provider have not yet been confirmed; no fixed support-retention period is promised in this draft.

Your choices and deletion

  • Delete Nudge History in Settings → History & Privacy removes Nudge’s daily and weekly history and queues the deletion for the paired Watch to apply when it syncs. Walking plans, settings, and the forecast archive are stored separately.
  • Clear Imported Health Context clears Nudge’s imported health snapshots on the iPhone. If you leave Health access on, Nudge can import eligible data again. Rebuild All History from Apple Health explicitly imports available step history.
  • Both controls leave original records in Apple Health, including saved workouts. Manage those records and Nudge’s read or write access in Apple Health. You can change Nudge’s location permission in device settings and turn off Walking Forecast and its background location option in Nudge.
  • Additional U.S. alerts are off until you enable them. Turn them off at any time in Walking Weather Preferences to stop future automatic NOAA lookups. Apple Weather forecasts and available Apple alerts continue either way. Previously downloaded alerts can remain visible until they expire, and NOAA handles any records it already holds under its own policies.
  • Nudge marks its working Health-derived history and caches for exclusion from device backups. A separate recovery record may be included in a backup. It contains selected settings, walking plans, eligible goals, and a record of history deletion. It does not contain imported steps, workout details, sleep, or heart readings. You can rebuild eligible step history from Apple Health if you still have access to it. Apple Health’s own sync and backup follow your Apple settings. Deleting history in Nudge does not erase temporary exports, shared copies, or older backups, which may contain data saved by earlier versions. Access, correction, and complaints

Email hello@nudgestep.com to request access to or correction of information we hold, ask for deletion where applicable, withdraw consent for future use, or make a privacy complaint. The proposed request-handling process is to review your request, confirm identity where needed, and respond with the outcome. The responsible contact and operating process require owner confirmation before this policy becomes effective. We can also help you use the controls for data stored on your device. You may contact the privacy regulator that covers your location. Canada and provincial regulators · Australia’s OAIC · New Zealand’s Privacy Commissioner · U.S. FTC
