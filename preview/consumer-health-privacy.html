Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Proposed policy — pending final approval. Not yet effective.

Prepared for Nudge 2.1’s U.S. release, including Washington and Nevada where their consumer-health laws apply. Effective date: to be set after approval.

Nudge is provided by Christopher Peluso, independent app developer. Privacy requests and appeals: hello@nudgestep.com.

Information and purposes

Nudge processes the following information to show progress and history, estimate walking time, and personalize the walking guidance you choose:

  • Activity and Health records: steps, walking/running distance, movement and exercise time, walking speed and step length, flights climbed, active energy, workouts and routes, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, and heart-rate variability, read from Apple Health with your permission.
  • Walking preferences and derived information: goals, plans, settings, feedback, progress, walking-time estimates, and coaching insights, supplied by you or calculated on your devices from your activity.
  • Weather locations: current device coordinates or saved Home coordinates from a postal-code lookup, used for walking forecasts and optional weather alerts. These are described here where they qualify as consumer health data.
  • Voluntary support information: health-related details, if any, that you choose to include in an email, used to respond to your request.

Where processing and sharing happen

Health records and walking history are processed locally, including inferences used for guidance. Paired iPhone and Apple Watch exchange history and settings; widgets use local data. There are no Nudge user accounts or developer-operated health-data servers. The app does not send your steps, goals, workouts, or Apple Health records to Christopher Peluso.

For weather, your device sends forecast coordinates to Apple WeatherKit; iPhone location lookup sends places or coordinates to Apple’s geocoding service. If you enable Additional U.S. alerts, your device sends the Current or saved Home forecast point directly to the National Weather Service (NOAA), including eligible background requests. NOAA receives your IP address and a Nudge-identifying request header. These requests contain no Apple Health records. NOAA’s retention of the coordinates is not confirmed. Apple privacy information · NOAA privacy notice.

Support emails and attachments reach Christopher Peluso through email delivery and storage providers. Exports go only to recipients or services you choose. No affiliates receive consumer health data. Nudge does not sell consumer health data or enable advertising or analytics services to collect it across websites or online services. External providers handle their requests under their own published practices; this is not a promise about unrelated activity on their services.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests and resolve related issues.

Your controls and their limits

You can review history in Nudge and original records in Apple Health, and change goals and preferences in Nudge. There is no general editor for imported historical Health values. History Export includes daily summaries and your Walking Plan, not every local data category.

  • Withdraw Health or location permissions in Apple’s settings, or turn off Walking Forecast, background location, or Additional U.S. alerts in Nudge. Withdrawal stops the related future access or requests; it does not erase retained history, Apple Health records, or provider records.
  • Settings → History & Privacy → Delete Nudge History deletes daily and weekly history and queues deletion for the paired Watch when it next syncs. Clear Imported Health Context separately clears the iPhone’s imported snapshot archive. With permission, eligible Health data can be imported again.
  • Plans, goals, settings, feedback, forecast archives, and other local stores are outside that history-delete control. Exports, prior backups, and original Apple Health records are not remotely erased. A backup-eligible recovery record retains selected preferences, eligible goals/plans, and deletion checkpoints.

Requests and appeals

Where applicable, you may request confirmation and access, a list of recipients, withdrawal of consent, or deletion. Email hello@nudgestep.com with the action requested and your state. You may also ask to review or correct information supplied through support. No account is required. We use reasonable verification, usually through your existing correspondence; do not send identity documents or Health exports unless specifically needed and agreed.

We can act on information we hold and help you use local controls. We cannot remotely inspect or erase your devices. A request extending beyond those controls will be assessed individually, with an explanation of limitations and any required further action.

Applicable Washington requests are handled within 45 days of receipt; Nevada requests within 45 days of authentication, with Nevada deletion due within 30 days of authentication. A permitted extension will be explained before the original response deadline.

If we decline a request, reply to the same address with “Privacy appeal” and explain what you want reconsidered. We will provide a written decision and reasons within 45 days of receiving the appeal. If denied, we will include a way to contact the relevant Attorney General: Washington or Nevada.

Changes to this notice

Material changes will be identified at the top of this page with an updated effective date and a notice on the website homepage. This proposed notice is not yet effective.
