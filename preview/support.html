Getting started

Which devices can I use?

Use a supported iPhone running iOS 17 or later. Apple Watch features require watchOS 10 or later. In 2.1, choose Apple Watch tracking for the wrist experience, or Apple Health tracking on iPhone without a Watch.

Why are my steps missing or different?

Nudge uses Apple Health data. On iPhone, open Health → Sharing → Apps and Services → Nudge and review access to Steps. Names can vary with your iOS version. Counts can differ while devices sync or Apple Health combines sources. Missing permission or incomplete history is not the same as zero activity.

How do I install Nudge on my Watch?

Open the Watch app on your paired iPhone, find Nudge under Available Apps, and choose Install. Open Nudge on the Watch and review its Health permissions. Keep both apps updated and give the devices time to sync.

Where do I change reminders?

In Nudge 2.1, open Settings → Notifications. Also check the device’s notification permissions and Focus settings. Some notification types and delivery behavior differ between iPhone and Watch.

Nudge+ subscriptions

How do I restore or manage Nudge+?

In Nudge 2.1, use the Nudge+ access controls in Settings and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Manage Subscription opens Apple’s subscription controls; you can also manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Which plan has a free trial?

The annual plan offers seven days free for eligible Apple Accounts, then US $19.99 per year unless canceled at least 24 hours before the trial ends. The US $2.99 monthly plan has no trial. Both have the same features and renew automatically unless canceled. Apple confirms price and eligibility in the app.

Will canceling delete my progress?

No. Free includes your underlying history, browsing, export, and deletion controls. Deeper insights and coaching reviews require active Nudge+. Your saved weekday plan is retained; after renewal, you can choose whether to restore it.

Walking weather

Why can’t I see Weather?

Walking Weather is a Nudge+ feature in 2.1. Free has no weather tab, weather card, or weather requests. With active Nudge+, review Walking Forecast and your location preferences in Settings.

How are walking conditions personalized?

Set your preferences for temperature, rain, wind, daylight, and UV. Nudge uses the forecast and those preferences to identify walking windows. A favorable rating is guidance, not a guarantee of safe conditions. Check available official alerts and your surroundings.

How often does the forecast update?

Normal automatic and manual requests share an allowance of 30 successful refreshes per local day. Failed requests do not use that allowance. Current forecasts become eligible on app opening after 60 minutes, or on Weather entry/resume after 30 minutes. Pull to refresh normally has a 30-minute cooldown.

Background timing depends on the operating system; eligibility is not a promise of delivery. An ordinary failed automatic request can delay automatic retries for three hours. An eligible manual refresh remains available. The last saved forecast stays visible when an update fails.

Can I check Home while traveling?

Yes. Home while Away refreshes are manual-only, at least three hours apart, with up to three successful updates each day included in the shared daily allowance. Returning Home shows the cached Home forecast; arrival itself does not fetch a new one.

What location information leaves my device?

Apple WeatherKit receives the coordinates used for forecasts; Apple’s geocoding service can receive a place or coordinates for location lookup. If you enable Additional U.S. alerts, the forecast point is also sent directly to NOAA. This optional feature starts off. Read the proposed Nudge 2.1 privacy policy, pending final approval.

Your history & privacy

Can I export or delete my history without paying?

Yes. In 2.1, open Settings → History & Privacy. Prepare History Export creates a file you can share. Delete Nudge History removes Nudge’s daily and weekly history and queues the deletion for the paired Watch. Walking plans, settings, and forecast archives are separate.

Deleting Nudge history does not delete original Apple Health records or previously shared exports. Clear Imported Health Context removes imported health snapshots on iPhone; allowed data may be imported again while Health access remains enabled.

Can I use Nudge without an account?

Nudge has no separate account or developer-operated cloud history service. Apple handles App Store purchases. You control Health and location permissions and choose whether to share exports.
