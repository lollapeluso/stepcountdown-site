How do I find a good time to walk?

Walking Weather is a Nudge+ feature that compares forecasts with your preferences.

  1. Open Weather, then open its settings to review your weather preferences and walking hours.
  2. Choose a day to see its summary and best available walking window.
  3. Review the Next 3 Hours and hourly details before heading out.
  4. Tap Why this rating? where offered to understand which conditions affect the result.

Ideal is the closest fit; Good may involve a small compromise. Fair or Poor conditions fit your preferences less well. Ratings describe forecast fit, not guaranteed safety. Check your surroundings and official warnings.

The best window and its Ideal portion are not necessarily identical. A rating can describe the weather while the hour lies outside your chosen walking hours. Walking Weather Hours and daylight preferences shape weather suggestions; Finish By still guides daily coaching.
