How do I use Nudge on Apple Watch?

Use your paired Watch for wrist progress, walking timing and available coaching.

  1. In the Watch app on your iPhone, find Nudge under Available Apps and install it if needed.
  2. Open Nudge on the Watch and review its Health permissions.
  3. Choose Apple Watch tracking in Nudge’s iPhone Settings if that is how you want to use Nudge.
  4. Explore step progress, walking time, Step Timer, history and Settings; Nudge+ adds walking weather.

Step Timer supports a focused walking session with a step target and an indoor/outdoor choice. Follow the Watch prompts to start and end a session. Additional workout permissions may be requested.

Keep both apps updated. Progress, plans and access can take time to synchronize; opening both apps while connected can help, but immediate delivery is not guaranteed.
