How do I get started with Nudge?

Nudge helps you work toward a daily step goal, see your progress and find a walking routine that fits your day.

  1. Open Nudge on your iPhone and follow the setup screens. This guide covers the upcoming 2.1; the public App Store currently offers 1.3.
  2. Choose Apple Watch tracking if you use a paired Watch, or Apple Health tracking to use the activity available on your iPhone.
  3. Review the requested Health permissions, then choose a starting step goal. You can choose a goal even if no step history is available.
  4. Continue with Free or review the optional Nudge+ plans. You can revisit your choice in Settings.

You do not need a Nudge account. A Watch is optional. Updates from other trackers depend on those apps sharing steps with Apple Health.
