How do Current location and Home work?

Nudge+ can show weather for your current area or a saved Home location.

  1. Review the location and Home options in Walking Forecast settings.
  2. Allow the location access needed for your chosen mode, or review your saved Home lookup.
  3. When away, check which location is selected before reading a forecast.

Home while Away updates are manual, with at least three hours between successful updates and up to three per day within the shared weather allowance. Returning Home can show its cached forecast; arrival itself does not guarantee a new request.

Location lookup and forecasts involve Apple services. Optional Additional U.S. alerts send the forecast coordinates to NOAA. Neither service request includes your Health records.
