What is included in Free and Nudge+?

Free covers everyday step tracking. Nudge+ adds walking weather, weekday plans and deeper coaching.

Free

A daily goal shared across the week, temporary daily-goal adjustments, step progress, underlying activity history, history browsing, export and deletion controls, essential coaching and a monthly summary. The Watch includes step progress and Step Timer. Basic step widgets remain useful without a subscription.

Nudge+

Personalized walking weather; separate goals for each weekday; history-based plan suggestions and monthly coaching reviews; deeper insights and activity comparisons; enhanced coaching and weather-enabled widget views. Free does not show the Weather tab or make weather requests.

If access ends, your history is retained. Your saved weekday plan is preserved; when access returns, you can choose whether to restore it rather than replacing your current plan automatically.
