How do I review my history and insights?

Your activity history remains available in Free; Nudge+ adds deeper comparisons and coaching.

  1. On Steps, find History and use the week controls to explore recorded activity.
  2. Use the history browser to explore earlier records.
  3. With Nudge+, open Insights for activity comparisons and consistency views. Choose the available period and move between date ranges.

Consistency can compare days against the same target or their recorded daily goals. A same-target comparison does not rewrite past goals or achievements. Missing days are not automatically treated as zero activity. Imported Health activity may not have a known historical Nudge goal.

Monthly Summary is available in Free. Nudge+ Monthly Review adds personalized plan choices. Reviewing a suggestion does not save a new plan until you confirm it.
