How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

Manage billing through Apple; restore access inside Nudge.

  1. To manage billing, open Settings → Nudge+ Access → Manage Subscription when shown, or open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Nudge.
  2. To restore an existing purchase, use the Apple Account that purchased it, then choose Restore Purchases in Nudge+ Access.
  3. Read the result. If confirmation remains unresolved, use the access-check option shown rather than buying again.

Canceling renewal does not delete your walking history. Reinstalling the app does not cancel a subscription and is not a recommended first troubleshooting step. Nudge checks Apple-verified access after installation or upgrade; availability can take time to confirm.
