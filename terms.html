Nudge+ subscriptions

Nudge+ is coming with version 2.1. U.S. pricing is $2.99 per month or $19.99 per year. Both plans include the same Nudge+ features.

The annual plan offers a seven-day free trial for eligible Apple Accounts, then renews at $19.99 per year unless canceled at least 24 hours before the trial ends. The monthly plan has no free trial. Eligibility and purchase terms are confirmed by Apple before purchase.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account subscription settings. Apple handles billing and refund requests.
