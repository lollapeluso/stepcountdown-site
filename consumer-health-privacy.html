Your privacy

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026.

This policy covers Nudge, including version 2.1. Features described apply when available in your version and enabled.

Nudge is provided by Christopher Peluso, independent app developer. Contact: hello@nudgestep.com.

Information Nudge uses

With your permission, Nudge reads Apple Health activity, workouts and routes, sleep, and heart measurements. Activity includes steps, distance, movement time, walking speed, step length, flights climbed and active energy.

Your goals, plans, preferences and feedback, together with insights calculated on your devices, help Nudge show progress and personalize walking guidance. Weather locations come from your device or a saved Home lookup.

On your devices and external services

Health records and walking history stay on your devices, including your paired Apple Watch and widgets. Nudge has no user accounts or developer-operated health database. The app does not send your steps, goals, workouts or Health records to Christopher Peluso.

For forecasts and place lookup, your device sends locations to Apple WeatherKit and geocoding services. Enabling optional NOAA alerts sends forecast coordinates, potentially precise, to the National Weather Service. These requests include your IP address, but no Health records. See Apple’s and NOAA’s privacy notices for their practices.

Support emails share whatever you include with Christopher Peluso and email delivery/storage providers to respond to you. Exports go to recipients you choose. No affiliates receive consumer health data. Nudge does not sell it or use advertising or analytics services to track it across websites or services.

Storage and your controls

Local retention varies by data type; some history and settings have no automatic expiry. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests and resolve related issues.

You can review history, change goals/preferences, and withdraw Health/location permissions or disable weather and NOAA alerts. Withdrawal does not delete existing records.

Delete Nudge History removes daily/weekly history, including on the paired Watch when it next syncs. Clear Imported Health Context clears imported snapshots separately. These controls do not erase every setting, plan, forecast, export, backup or original Apple Health record.

Requests and rights

Where Washington or Nevada consumer-health laws apply, you may request confirmation/access, recipient information, withdrawal of consent or deletion without discrimination. Email hello@nudgestep.com with your request and state. You may also request review/correction of support information. We verify requests reasonably and respond within applicable legal deadlines.

We can address information we hold and help with device controls, but cannot remotely inspect or erase your devices. Requests beyond those controls receive individual review.

To appeal a refusal, reply “Privacy appeal.” We will provide a written decision within 45 days, with reasons and the relevant Attorney General’s contact if denied.

Material policy changes will be announced here and on the website homepage, with an updated effective date.
