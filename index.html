A few helpful answers

Before your
next step.

Can I download Nudge 2.1 now?

Not yet. Nudge 2.1 is coming soon. The App Store currently offers Nudge 1.3; its features differ from those shown here.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

Nudge 2.1 supports Apple Health tracking on iPhone without a Watch. An Apple Watch adds wrist-based progress, walking tools, and complications.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Essential tracking, reminders, history, basic monthly summaries, and all Watch complications are Free. Nudge+ adds walking weather, deeper insights, and personalized planning.

Does Nudge replace Apple Workouts?

Nudge helps you plan around your daily step goal. Apple Workouts records individual workouts; the current Watch Walk Plan can open a suggested walk there.

What happens if I cancel Nudge+?

Access continues through your verified paid period. After it ends, Free features remain available. Your underlying history is retained; deeper analysis and new weather updates require Nudge+.
