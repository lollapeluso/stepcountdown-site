How do I add complications or Smart Stack widgets?

Watch complications put Nudge information on a compatible watch face.

  1. Touch and hold your watch face, choose Edit and select a supported complication position. Choose Nudge from the available options.
  2. Steps to Go supports corner, circular, rectangular and inline positions. Minutes Left supports corner and circular positions.
  3. For Smart Stack, open the stack, touch and hold to edit, then add Nudge’s available rectangular widget. Walking Forecast requires Nudge+ for weather.

The face and watchOS determine which positions are offered. Walking Forecast is rectangular; Steps to Go also has a rectangular presentation. Updates are scheduled by watchOS rather than streamed continuously.

Apple’s Watch guide explains face and Smart Stack controls.
