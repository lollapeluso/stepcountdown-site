Which iPhone widgets can I add?

Choose a compact progress view or a larger overview from the Nudge widget gallery.

  1. Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen and open the widget gallery. Search for Nudge.
  2. Choose a style and size, then add it to your Home Screen.
  3. Open Nudge to make sure current activity and any requested weather are available.

Home Screen gallery

Small: Steps to Go. Medium: Steps to Go, Steps + This Week, Steps + Consistency, and Walking Forecast. Large: Daily Overview, Daily Overview + This Month, Daily Overview + 3-Month Consistency, Steps + Consistency + Forecast, and Steps + Recent Steps + Forecast.

Weather panels require Nudge+. A gallery entry is not a promise that every panel is available in Free. Widgets can show saved information until iOS grants a refresh.

Apple’s widget instructions cover adding and rearranging widgets.
