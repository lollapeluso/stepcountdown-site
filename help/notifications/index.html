How do I choose reminders and notifications?

Reminder choices depend on your tracking mode and Free or Nudge+ access.

  1. Open Settings → Notifications in Nudge.
  2. Review All Notifications and the iPhone or Apple Watch options shown for your tracking mode.
  3. Free includes morning reminder and monthly summary options. Nudge+ adds adaptive morning and evening check-ins and enhanced coaching options.
  4. Also check Nudge’s notification permission in your device Settings and any active Focus.

Watch mode offers wrist alerts; Apple Health mode offers iPhone coaching. Delivery is subject to system scheduling and available activity. Changing a preference does not guarantee an immediate alert. Weather alerts have separate location choices and require Nudge+.
