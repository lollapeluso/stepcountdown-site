How do I connect Apple Health?

Nudge uses the Apple Health information you allow it to read.

  1. Review Nudge’s Health permission request during setup. Allow Steps for step progress and Workouts for workout information.
  2. To review access later, open Apple Health → Sharing → Apps and Services → Nudge. The wording can vary by iOS version.
  3. If you use another tracker, check that its app writes steps to Apple Health.
  4. Open Nudge again after changing permissions and allow time for the data to become available.

Missing permission, a locked device or unavailable history can prevent a reading. An empty result does not always mean you walked zero steps. You remain in control of permissions; disabling them does not delete previously saved records.
