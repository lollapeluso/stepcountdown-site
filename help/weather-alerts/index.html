How do I use weather alerts?

Nudge+ can display available official weather alerts and offer weather notifications.

  1. Review Walking Forecast settings and the optional Additional U.S. alerts choice. NOAA alerts start off.
  2. In Notifications, review Weather alerts for Current location and Home.
  3. Allow system notifications if you want eligible alerts delivered.

NOAA is an optional source of additional U.S. alert information; coverage and detail vary. Open the alert to read available official details. Not every forecast change produces a notification, and background delivery is not guaranteed. Do not rely on Nudge as your only emergency warning source.
