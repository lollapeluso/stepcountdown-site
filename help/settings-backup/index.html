How do I manage preferences and protect my data?

Settings brings together Walking Plan, notifications, tracking mode and history controls.

Choose your week start and walking pace settings to suit your routine. With Nudge+, review weather preferences and Lock Screen widget mode. Changing tracking mode affects which device supplies the coaching experience; it does not grant Health permission automatically.

The Protect Your Nudge Data card explains backups. A device backup that includes Nudge can help recover selected settings, plans and eligible goals. Nudge’s copy of Apple Health history is excluded from backups; available Health activity can be used to rebuild step history.

Before replacing or erasing a device, read that guidance and verify your backup. Do not uninstall Nudge as a routine fix for missing steps or purchases.
