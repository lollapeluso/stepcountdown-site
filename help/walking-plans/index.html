How do I set or change my walking goal?

Start with a manageable target. You can change it as your routine changes.

  1. Open Settings → Walking Plan.
  2. In Free, choose Edit Daily Goal to set one target for every day. With Nudge+, choose Edit My Plan to set weekday targets.
  3. Review your changes and choose Save. Nudge+ history suggestions remain editable and do not change your plan until saved.

Nudge+ also offers Edit today’s goal for a one-day override and Use my planned goal to return to the plan. Plan changes apply from today; earlier recorded daily goals stay saved. Missed steps do not become extra work tomorrow. A walking focus guides coaching without automatically changing your goals.
