Can I make my goal easier for a few days?

Temporary adjustments are available in both Free and Nudge+.

  1. Open Settings → Walking Plan → Adjust Steps Temporarily.
  2. Choose the adjustment and its duration, then review the preview. Free uses one temporary daily goal; Nudge+ adjusts your weekday rhythm.
  3. Save the adjustment. Use Edit Adjustment or End Now if your plans change.

Your regular goal returns when the adjustment ends. Check the preview before saving so the affected days and targets are what you intended.
