Help Center / Privacy & troubleshooting

What should I check when something is missing?

Start with permissions, connection and the time shown on the screen.

Missing or different steps

Check whether the steps are present in Apple Health and whether Nudge can read them. Unlock the device and reopen Nudge. Other trackers may not have shared their latest records yet; do not replace missing history with zero or delete it to force a refresh.

iPhone and Watch disagree

Open both apps, check their connection and give them time to synchronize. Confirm both have Health permission and that you chose the intended tracking mode. Plans and Nudge+ access may arrive separately from activity.

Weather or widgets look old

Check Nudge+ access, location, connectivity and the forecast reference time. Open Weather and refresh when available. Opening the app can make newer information available to widgets, but iOS and watchOS decide when to display it. Older weather can remain visible after an unsuccessful update.

Still stuck?

Email hello@nudgestep.com with the app version, device model, OS version and a brief description. Remove health details and precise locations from screenshots. Never send passwords, payment details or a full Health export.
