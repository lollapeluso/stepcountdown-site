How do I set up the Lock Screen widget?

Nudge offers the rectangular Walking Overview widget on iPhone.

  1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, choose Customize, then select the Lock Screen.
  2. Tap the widget area and add Nudge’s Walking Overview.
  3. With Nudge+, review Lock Screen Widget in Nudge Settings to choose Auto, Forecast, Steps or Activity.

Auto schedules rotation among views; when forecast content is unavailable it can fall back to steps. A scheduled change is not a guarantee of an immediate visible refresh. The operating system controls when widgets render and may hide protected information while locked.

Use the widget to open the app when you need fuller or more recent information.
