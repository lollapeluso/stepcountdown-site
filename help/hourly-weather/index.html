How do I read hourly and daily forecasts?

Use the day selector for planning and hourly details for the conditions around a walk.

Choose a day to review its walking summary and available windows. The Next 3 Hours describes the actual upcoming period. Select an hour in the timeline to inspect feels-like temperature, precipitation, wind, UV, visibility and light conditions when available.

Walking hours focuses the timeline on your chosen hours; Full day gives a wider view. Times follow the forecast location. Current observed conditions and hourly forecast measurements can have different reference times; read the explanation on the card.

Forecasts are estimates and can change. An older saved forecast is not a fresh observation just because you reopened the screen.
