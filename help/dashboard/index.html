What does the Steps screen show?

The Steps screen brings together your goal, progress and recent activity.

Your daily card shows steps toward today’s target and celebrates when you meet it. Walking time is an estimate based on your steps-per-minute setting, not a promise about how long a walk will take. Today’s progress helps show when you were active; History lets you review previous days and weeks.

With Nudge+, a walking-weather card can point to a suitable window. Open Weather for the full forecast. Availability depends on permissions, saved data and confirmed subscription access.

Finish By helps guide goal timing and reminders. It is separate from Walking Weather Hours, which describe when you want weather suggestions.
