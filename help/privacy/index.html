Where is my information stored, and how do I control it?

Nudge processes walking and Health information on your devices without a developer-operated health database or Nudge user account.

Your iPhone, paired Watch and widgets share the information needed for their features. Apple services receive weather/location requests; optional NOAA alerts also use forecast coordinates. Apple manages subscriptions. Support receives only what you choose to include in an email.

  1. Review Apple Health and location permissions in your device settings.
  2. In Nudge, open Settings → History & Privacy for export and deletion controls.
  3. Read each confirmation before deleting: deleting Nudge daily/weekly history and clearing imported Health context are separate actions.

These controls do not remove all plans, preferences, saved forecasts, backups, exports or original Apple Health records. Revoking permission stops allowed access but does not erase existing records.

Read the official Privacy Policy and Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for details and privacy requests.
