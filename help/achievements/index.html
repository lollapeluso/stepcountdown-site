How do goals and achievements work?

Nudge recognizes progress toward your own walking goals.

Progress and goal-met celebrations use the goal recorded for that day. Historical achievements are tied to recorded activity and goals; a new target does not mean every old day is judged against it. A consistency comparison against one target is a separate way to explore your activity.

Steps may arrive later from Apple Health or a paired device, so history can take time to settle. A notification is not required for a goal to count. If a past result looks wrong, contact support with the date and a short description rather than deleting your history.
