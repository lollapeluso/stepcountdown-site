What if Nudge+ does not activate?

An unresolved purchase confirmation is not the same as a confirmed failed purchase.

  1. Keep an internet connection available and read the status shown in Nudge.
  2. If the purchase screen shows Check Purchase Access, tap it to recheck the existing purchase. This does not start a new purchase.
  3. In Settings → Nudge+ Access, Check Access may be offered for unresolved access. Restore Purchases asks Apple to synchronize purchases.
  4. If a check cannot finish, reopen Nudge and try again later. Contact support if access still disagrees with your Apple subscription status.

Avoid repeatedly purchasing or redeeming while confirmation is pending. Nudge cannot verify access until Apple supplies a valid result. Do not delete the app or reset history to fix billing; developer mode is not required.
